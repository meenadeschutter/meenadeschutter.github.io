# Brand & words

## Name and voice
- Site name: **Meena De Schutter** ("Meena" in navy, "De Schutter" in link blue).
- Voice: gentle and sincere, self-aware and funny. Show the hobbies in their sections rather than listing them in copy.

## Homepage copy (decided)
- Headline: **I make things for phones & for fun.** ("phones" in blue, "fun" on a powder-blue highlight)
- Subtitle: Puzzle nerd and hobby hopper. Once something catches my interest, I get completely obsessed, and you won't see me for a few months while I figure out how it all fits together.
- Titles:
  - **By day,** Senior Mobile Developer (powder-blue highlight, straight)
  - **By night,** Chaotic Creator (navy highlight, tilted −1.5°; the mismatch is on purpose)
- About heading: A bit of everything, held together by curiosity.
- About body: I'm a software engineer who's worked in Canada, the Netherlands, Czechia and California, and I love learning new things. With every new hobby, I get good enough to be delusional enough to compete against the best. I usually land somewhere respectable, rarely on the podium, and always have an absolute blast, because at the end of the day, it's about the fun.
- Hobby sections are numbered **pieces** (never "issues"), each with a specific link: "see all lenses →", "play the games →", "see the makes →", "see all times →", "flip through →".
- Footer: "pieced together by Meena".

## Visual system (the base never changes)
- Colors: cream `#FBF7EF`, powder blue `#CFE0EF` (+ `#E3ECF5`, `#A9C6E2`), navy `#1F2A44`, link blue `#2C5BB8`.
- Type: Bricolage Grotesque (headings and body), DM Mono (labels), Kalam (one hand-drawn touch at a time).
- Every piece uses the same frame: a rounded card with a "no. 0X · name" header row.
- Each piece gets one statement inside its frame:
  - lenses: two phones and a giant 1.07M
  - games: navy pixel grid, Silkscreen font, blinking "press start"
  - sewing: pattern-paper grid and dashed cut lines
  - puzzles: navy timer card with best time and competitions
  - doodles: white paper, Kalam headline, slightly tilted sketches
  - apps & work: code-snippet card and experience list
- Hero: six jigsaw pieces with icons (phone, sparkles, controller, scissors, stopwatch, pencil) that snap together on click.

## Restraint rules
1. New things go into an existing piece, or become a new piece in the same frame. Never a new style on the homepage.
2. One statement per piece. A second trick goes on that piece's own page.
3. The base palette is closed: no new colors outside a piece's frame.
4. Wild experiments live on each piece's own page.

## Alive and lived-in
- "On the workbench" (in-progress items with progress bars and an "updated" date) and a "Recently" log.
